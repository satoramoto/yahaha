# Where yahaha stands (2026-10-19)

A landing report after the project sat idle while syns, zen and BLOOM took the focus. Read-only sweep of `develop`, every branch, worktree and PR, and the zen side. Numbers and checks are from this day.

## 1. What yahaha is, and what finished means

In the repo's own words: yahaha is "a software arranger keyboard. It loads Yamaha Genos/PSR/Tyros style files and follows the chords you play on a MIDI keyboard. The band plays through a built-in SoundFont synth, and also out of a virtual MIDI port" (README). Its scope is "**playability**. Load Genos style files and play them the way the hardware does. There are no editors in scope" (docs/backlog.md). The release target is v1 as docs/eyes-free.md defines it: "a signed desktop build (and the iPad build) handed to the owner and a few friends. It is not a public release: no notarized DMG, no onboarding for strangers, no shipped content." Feature freeze is everything on `develop`. **Done when:** "a friend with a Mac (or iPad), a Launchkey MK4, a folder of styles and a GM font installs the build and jams for a set without the owner beside them; and the owner plays a set on it without looking at the screen." The eyes-free plan's eight steps are the path: steps 0–5 (mixer/rack, sound numbers, hold-Sound, swap mode, capture, Racks and Setup pages) landed on 2026-09-30; step 6 (a week of sets) is the owner's and has not happened; step 7 (visual polish) grew into the Push redesign and UI rewrite; step 8 (release: `develop` → `main`, tag `v0.1.0`, signed builds) has not started.

Milestones M1–M8 on GitHub (chord recognition, chord-to-note, engine API and Tauri shell, style browser, sections and transport, parts and voices, registration/Multi Pads/playlist, iReal charts) are all closed. PLAN.md's older M0–M5 table is superseded by them. The 36 open issues are 28 `spec` issues (one Push board each, #506–#535 less the ones done; #513 Browser is done in #541 but still open) and 24 engine or app follow-ups, none blocking.
